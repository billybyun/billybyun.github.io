# Content Strategy

## Audience
Robotics / multimodal AI teams, research engineering roles, and neurotech/BCI groups.

## What goes where
### Demos
Concrete artifacts:
- repo link + short demo gif/video (later)
- what it does + why it matters
- key result / metric (if any)
- roadmap: next step

### Concepts
Synthesis pages:
- architecture diagrams (simple)
- definitions + core mechanisms
- failure modes + open questions
- “how I would extend this”

### Lit Reviews
Paper-specific notes:
- 5–8 bullet summary (not a long essay)
- diagram of architecture (when helpful)
- what’s novel
- limitations
- connection to my demos/themes

## Quality Bar (must-have)
- One clear takeaway per page
- One diagram OR one pseudo-pipeline
- One “why it matters” line
- One “limitations / next” line

## Publishing Cadence
- 1 concept page / week OR 1 lit review / week
- Keep landing page curated (only top 3–6 items)
